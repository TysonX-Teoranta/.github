## Who we are

TysonX Teoranta is a private Irish software company, incorporated in 2026 and based in Limerick. We design, build, and run our own web products end to end — from the first line of code to the live service.

We favour small, focused teams, careful engineering, and software that earns trust by being dependable rather than loud. That is what *silent success* means to us.

## What we build

| Product | What it is | Status |
|---------|-----------|--------|

## How we work

- **Built in Ireland.** Designed and operated locally, for an Irish-first audience and beyond.
- **Modern stack.** .NET, Blazor, PostgreSQL, and a continuous-delivery pipeline that keeps releases safe and steady.
- **Quality over noise.** We ship when it's right, we test before we trust, and we keep our software honest.

## The name

TysonX is named after a dog — a Patterdale terrier, loyal and watchful. He's on our logo, and he's the standard we hold our software to: shows up every day, asks for little, never lets you down.
